About

About 256 Newsroom

256 Newsroom helps readers discover current reporting from Ugandan, regional and international publishers. Aggregated reports lead to independently written summary pages with clear source attribution and a link to the publisher's complete report.

Editorial Standards

Summaries must accurately represent the available source material, distinguish reported facts from context, identify the original publisher and avoid invented quotations, figures or claims. Corrections should be made promptly and transparently.

Corrections Policy

Readers, journalists and publishers can report an error by emailing editor@256newsroom.com. Include the story URL and the specific statement requiring review.

Fact-Checking Policy

Automated summaries are marked for later editorial review. A flag does not remove a page automatically; confirmed errors are corrected, updated or withdrawn. Source attribution and original-report links remain visible.

Privacy Policy

The site may store reader preferences locally to personalize categories and districts. Account information is used to provide newsroom access and is not sold. Precise location access requires browser permission.

Terms of Service

Users must not submit unlawful, deceptive, plagiarized or harmful material. Publishers and journalists retain responsibility for content they submit. 256 Newsroom may correct, restrict or remove material that breaches these terms.

Copyright

Original reports remain the property of their publishers. 256 Newsroom provides attribution, limited summaries and links to complete reports. Rights holders can contact the editorial team about images, excerpts or attribution.

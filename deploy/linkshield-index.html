            ● FAQ

            Free URL scanner — questions answered

            Is the scanner free? Do I need an account?

            Yes — completely free. No account, no sign-up. Paste a link and scan instantly.

            What do the verdicts mean?

            Verified Authentic — listed in our trust registry with ownership reviewed by 256 AI Systems. Unrated — not enough verified evidence to score the page (like an unknown site). Caution / Community Reports — only shown when we have observable evidence or documented reports. We never claim malware, blacklisting, or official warnings without proof.

            How reliable is the result?

            It is a strong signal, not a guarantee. We only display warnings backed by evidence — community reports, HTTP errors, redirect mismatches, or trust registry records. When in doubt, verify through the official organization directly.

            Do you store what I scan?

            By default, scans are saved locally in your browser so you can copy a share link. Tick Don't store this scan before scanning and nothing is kept — you see the result once only.

            A site I trust was flagged — can I get it verified?

            Yes. Organizations can apply at Get Verified ($30 USD, convertible to local currency). We review ownership and add approved domains to the trust registry.
